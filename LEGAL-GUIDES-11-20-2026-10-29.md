## Editorial and source scope

Each article has topic-specific explanations, detailed tables, worked scenarios, FAQs, official service links and related reading. SEO includes a unique title and description, one H1, contents anchors, a self-referencing canonical URL, Open Graph/Twitter metadata, and Article and BreadcrumbList structured data. The publisher/author is H.R. Legal Associate as an Organization; no individual professional reviewer or live government verification is invented.

Official-site access remains unavailable under the managed network policy. Requests to UP RERA, IGRSUP, Lucknow district administration, LDA, India Code and the UP Board of Revenue returned HTTP 403. Newly issued 2026 rules, live project statuses, current female-purchaser concessions and BKT/Sitapur Road valuation figures have not been independently verified. The affected guides identify that limitation and direct readers to the authoritative service or operative notification. Numerical concession examples are explicitly assumed arithmetic, not a claim about current notified charges.

The articles explain established statutory principles and identify provisions where useful, without presenting an invented recent amendment or portal launch. Official destinations are supplied as reference links; their current content and availability could not be validated from this environment.
