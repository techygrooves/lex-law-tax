Motion

[data-reveal]

Elements marked data-reveal fade in and rise 18px over 620ms when they enter the viewport. Children of a data-reveal-group are staggered by 90ms, capped at 360ms.

The hidden state is applied only when site.js adds .js-reveal to the document element, and it does that only when IntersectionObserver exists and reduced motion is not requested. With prefers-reduced-motion: reduce nothing is ever hidden and no transition runs.
