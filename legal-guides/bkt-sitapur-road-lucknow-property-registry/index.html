BKT Lucknow property registry: identify the land before the rate

A property advertised as BKT, Bakshi Ka Talab or Sitapur Road can belong to a specific revenue village, authority scheme, private layout or built locality. For registry, the legal identity of the land matters more than the broad corridor name. Begin with the village, parcel or plot, area, jurisdiction and title documents.

Buyers in this corridor often compare residential plots, larger agricultural holdings and authority or private developments. Those products can require different title, revenue, planning and valuation checks even when the advertisements describe them all as “Lucknow plots”.

2026 local-rate note: This guide does not publish unverified BKT or Sitapur Road rupee-per-area rates. Obtain the applicable effective collector schedule from the Lucknow district administration and the UP registration department. The exact locality, road entry, use and property class determine the relevant lookup.

The useful goal is one connected file: the land shown on site, the owner's title, the applicable approval and the property described in the final deed should all match.

BKT, Sitapur Road and the revenue address

Bakshi Ka Talab is a recognised Lucknow tehsil and locality reference, while Sitapur Road is a broad corridor description. Neither expression alone identifies every parcel's registration office, planning authority or valuation entry. Check the competent jurisdiction against the actual property records.

A project may use a well-known road name while the underlying land lies inside a nearby revenue village. Keep the marketing address and legal address in separate fields until their connection is established. The sale deed should carry the identifiers needed to locate the property legally.

Documents to ask for at the first site visit

Obtain the seller's acquisition deed and the relevant earlier links, current revenue material where applicable, a site map and any claimed approval. Photograph or note the identifiers rather than accepting a verbal assurance that documents will be available after booking.

Compare the physical plot with the boundaries and access described. Ask who owns the approach road and whether it appears in the sanctioned layout or another legally sufficient access document. A pathway used during a sales visit does not establish a permanent right.

How to check the local circle-rate entry

Use the effective official valuation schedule for the relevant jurisdiction. Locate the exact village, locality, road or scheme entry and read its general instructions. Residential, commercial, agricultural, vacant and built property categories should not be collapsed into one corridor-wide figure.

Record the area unit and any prescribed location or construction factors. A per-square-metre entry cannot be applied directly to a square-foot area without conversion. Keep the relevant schedule pages and a reproducible worksheet with the transaction file.

Agricultural plotting and house construction

Where a small plot comes from agricultural land, establish whether the deed transfers a separate legally identified plot or only an undivided share. Examine the tenure, subdivision and planning position before describing the property as immediately residential.

A Section 80 declaration, where applicable, has a revenue-law function. It should be checked against the actual parcel, area and use, while layout and building requirements are examined separately. Read the agricultural land residential-use guide for the land-to-house sequence.

Do not infer approval from neighbouring construction or a development signboard. A house next door may have a different land history or permission position. Your file needs a parcel-specific answer.

LDA, other authority and RERA claims

Identify the competent planning body for the land from official jurisdiction and plan material. A BKT or Sitapur Road marketing address does not prove that a particular authority approved the layout. Request the actual sanction and covered land schedule.

Where RERA registration applies, match the promoter, project, phase and plot to the official record. A number belonging to an earlier phase is not automatically relevant to an extension. Our RERA verification checklist explains the comparison.

Road frontage, access and future widening

A road-facing location can influence price and use, but the buyer needs to distinguish actual access from a view of the main road. The connecting strip may be privately owned, reserved or subject to restrictions. Establish the lawful route to the property.

Check relevant official road or planning proposals where they affect the parcel. A salesperson's statement that a road “will become wider” is not the source for a property boundary or construction decision. Identify the actual reservation, notification or plan material and its scope.

A site inspection should note drains, existing walls, public facilities, occupied strips and the position of neighbouring plots. Where the ground and papers diverge, obtain the appropriate measurement or authority clarification before drafting a deed that assumes the discrepancy away.

A detailed example: two plots with the same corridor address

Plot A and Plot B are both advertised as “Sitapur Road, BKT”. Plot A is within a documented layout, has an identified road and a seller whose deed matches the plot. Plot B is shown on a private map, while the draft transfers an undivided share in a larger holding.

The asking price and broad address do not make these equivalent products. The buyer should compare the legal interest, approvals, access, possession and development obligations before comparing price per square foot.

Registry preparation and closing sequence

Once the title and approval file is coherent, prepare the sale deed with the exact property description, consideration, shares and possession terms. Calculate the government charges from the correct instrument and valuation basis. Preserve the assessment and official payment references.

Confirm the competent registration office, current application and attendance requirements, and documents needed for any representative. If a lender holds originals, agree the release and payment sequence before the appointment. A booking confirmation is not the registered conveyance.

Buying a built house or shop instead of an open plot

The corridor also includes constructed properties, where the file needs more than the underlying land record. Compare the building actually offered with the relevant sanctioned plan, use and property description. A deed that identifies land accurately may still leave building approval, occupancy or tenancy questions unanswered.

For a house, inspect the access, boundaries, construction extent and actual occupation. For a shop or mixed-use premises, establish the approved use and any conditions affecting the proposed business. Road visibility is a commercial advantage only if the property can lawfully support the intended activity.

A buyer should also distinguish an independent property from a unit created inside a larger building. The seller may offer a shop number or floor while the title documents refer to an undivided interest in the whole premises. Determine the legal basis of the exclusive unit and common access before drafting the conveyance.

Where possession is promised after registry, put the delivery date, occupation position and agreed consequences into the contract. A recital that possession has already been delivered should not be signed if the seller or tenant will remain under a different arrangement.

For valuation, describe the actual structure and use accurately. Paying an assessment does not regularise an unauthorised addition, but omitting construction from the file can also distort the transaction description and charge calculation. Keep approval and valuation findings in separate columns.

These checks are particularly useful when comparing an older house with a newly developed plot in the same locality. One may need renovation and tenant coordination; the other may need development works and a building route. The legal budget should reflect the product actually being bought rather than treating both as a generic square-foot purchase.

The final closing checklist should identify the land, structure, use, occupant and documents delivered. That creates a much clearer local registry plan than an assurance that the address is familiar and the papers are “complete”.

Frequently asked questions

Is there one circle rate for all BKT property?

No single corridor figure should be assumed. Identify the exact location, category, unit and effective schedule entry for the property.

Does a Sitapur Road address establish LDA approval?

No. Verify jurisdiction and the actual sanction covering the plot. A location description is not an approval document.

Can I buy an agricultural share and build on a marked corner?

Establish the legal interest, subdivision, use, access and building requirements first. A share and an exclusively owned separate plot are different rights.
