RERA project verification in Lucknow: start with the exact phase

RERA project verification in Lucknow means checking the proposed purchase against the official project record maintained under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016. The buyer needs to connect the registration number, promoter, project land, registered phase and particular apartment or plot. A RERA logo in an advertisement is only a starting point.

Use the Uttar Pradesh Real Estate Regulatory Authority as the official source for project registration and available disclosures. Search by the supported project, promoter or registration particulars, then compare the result with the documents supplied by the seller. Similar project names and adjoining phases should not be treated as interchangeable.

2026 status note: This guide does not certify the live status of any named project or claim a newly announced UP RERA procedure. Current registration, extensions, orders and disclosures must be checked in the official record when the purchase is being considered.

The practical outcome is a buyer file showing what is registered, what is approved, what is promised and what remains to be completed. These are related questions, but one registration entry does not answer all of them.

Collect a project identification sheet before searching

Ask the promoter for the full legal project name, registration number, phase, promoter name, land location and approval references. For an apartment, add tower, floor, unit number and the agreed area description. For a plot, add plot number, dimensions and the underlying revenue parcel connection.

Record the name used in advertisements separately from the legal name. A marketing brand can cover several entities, projects or phases. The agreement and official records should identify the actual party and property, not rely on brand recognition alone.

Step-by-step official project lookup

Open the official UP RERA website and select the available project-search service. Use the number supplied by the promoter where possible, and cross-check the project and promoter names in the result. Read the complete available project page rather than stopping when the search returns a match.

Examine the registration particulars and any status information, extension, order or linked document relevant to the purchase. Where the portal presents several records, identify the exact phase and registration. Save the source, search date and documents relied on in the buyer file.

If the promoter says the project is exempt from registration, ask for the legal and factual basis. Not every real-estate transaction falls within project registration requirements, but a private assertion of exemption is not a substitute for examining the statutory criteria and the project's facts.

Read approvals at parcel and unit level

Locate the layout or building sanction and compare its land schedule with the registered project. Identify the competent planning authority and the complete approved drawing, including revisions where applicable. A cropped approval letter does not show whether the offered plot or tower is inside the sanction.

For Lucknow projects, LDA approval, revenue conversion and RERA registration perform different roles. A Section 80 declaration does not independently approve a layout, and RERA registration does not cure a missing title link. Use our LDA plot verification guide for the drawing-to-ground checks.

Promoter title, mortgages and payment entitlement

Read the documents explaining the promoter's right to develop and sell. The arrangement may involve ownership, a development agreement, a landowner-promoter relationship or another structure. Identify the parties who must execute the agreement and conveyance for the rights being offered.

Investigate mortgages and the treatment of the particular unit or plot. If project land is charged to a lender, establish the applicable release or consent arrangement and how it will be documented at completion. A payment receipt from the promoter does not establish that lender rights have been discharged.

Check the payment recipient against the agreement and the official project information. An instruction to pay an unrelated personal account needs an authentic, lawful explanation before funds move. Preserve the price sheet, agreement, demand and payment references together.

Booking amount and agreement for sale

Section 13 of the central RERA framework restricts a promoter from taking more than ten per cent of the cost of the apartment, plot or building as an advance or application fee without first entering into the required written and registered agreement for sale. The agreement's required terms and the applicable UP framework should be examined for the transaction.

The agreement should accurately identify the property, price components, payment schedule, possession obligations and consequences of default or delay. A booking form is not a substitute for reading the operative agreement. Compare promises about parking, services, access and amenities with the documents that actually bind the parties.

Read updates and orders as a chronology

Look at the sequence of the project record: original registration, disclosed progress, extensions, relevant proceedings and later orders. A single status label may not explain the history or the obligations now in force. Read the operative documents behind important changes.

Where an order is cited by a salesperson, obtain the complete text and identify the project, parties, relief and conditions. A decision concerning another buyer, phase or issue should not be presented as an unrestricted approval of the entire development.

Record gaps for follow-up. Missing material can have several explanations, so ask for the document or official clarification that addresses the exact issue. The goal is an evidence-based purchase file, not a list of unexplained screenshots.

Example: a registered project number used for another phase

Assume a promoter advertises Green View Phase II and provides the registration number for Green View Phase I. The official record may be genuine while the match to the offered unit is wrong. Compare the land schedule, phase boundary and unit location before treating the number as relevant.

Buying a resale unit inside a registered project

A resale requires two connected investigations. The project record explains the development and its disclosures; the resale owner's documents explain the rights that person can sell. An original project registration number does not establish that the present seller completed their acquisition or can transfer the unit without a lender or other party's participation.

Ask for the seller's allotment, agreement, conveyance where applicable, possession documents and payment or dues records relevant to the interest offered. Identify whether the transaction is an assignment before conveyance or a sale of an already conveyed property. The correct instrument and necessary participation follow that distinction.

Where the promoter must participate in an assignment, obtain the applicable terms and written requirements. Where the owner already holds a conveyance, read it for restrictions and the rights included. Do not let a resale booking form obscure which legal transaction is proposed.

A buyer should also reconcile promises made by the resale seller with the project obligations. For example, an amenity claimed by the seller may belong to a different phase or depend on rights not included in the unit documents. Check the documentary basis rather than relying on the owner's description of how residents currently use it.

Keep project-level and owner-level findings in the same completion checklist, with separate action owners. A project approval issue may require promoter or authority records, while a seller mortgage needs that seller's lender arrangement. This prevents one party from saying the other side's documents answer an unrelated problem.

The final resale decision should therefore explain both the unit's place within the verified project and the seller's transferable interest. That combination is especially useful when a buyer is financing the purchase or expects immediate possession.

Frequently asked questions

Does RERA registration guarantee the property has clear title?

It is part of the disclosure and regulatory framework. Independently connect the title, development rights, approvals, security and specific unit to the transaction. Registration does not replace that legal analysis.

Can the same registration number cover every phase?

Check the actual official registration and phase particulars. Do not extend a number to adjoining or later land solely because the promoter uses one project brand.

Should I check the project again before the final payment?

Yes. Review material intervening updates, orders, approval changes and completion conditions. Keep the final decision tied to the current transaction rather than an old booking-stage search.

Where can I check a project advertised as exempt?

Examine the statutory basis, project facts and applicable official material. Ask the promoter for a documented explanation and use the authority's official enquiry route where clarification is needed.

Official sources and related guides

Use UP RERA for the official project record, LDA for relevant planning information and India Code for the central Act. Our Lucknow title verification guide explains the wider ownership review, and the plot buying checklist connects freehold, leasehold and approval checks.

A useful RERA verification result identifies the exact purchase, explains the documentary matches and turns unresolved issues into specific completion tasks. That gives a buyer something stronger than a registration badge: a transaction whose land, promoter, approvals and promises can be traced.
