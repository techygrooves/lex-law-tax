Section 80 Land Conversion in Uttar Pradesh 2026: Rules, Process and LDA Approval

Understand what a Section 80 declaration changes, how to prepare the land file, and why planning permission and LDA approval need separate examination.

What Section 80 land conversion in UP actually means

Section 80 of the Uttar Pradesh Revenue Code, 2006 concerns the declaration associated with qualifying agricultural holdings being used for specified non-agricultural purposes under the statutory framework. In property transactions, it is commonly described as agricultural-to-non-agricultural land conversion. The declaration must be understood through the land, tenure, order and legal provisions that actually apply.

For a Lucknow purchaser, the essential distinction is between the revenue treatment of the holding and permission to develop it. A Section 80 order is not a universal substitute for an approved layout, sanctioned building plan, lawful access or development authority permission.

2026 amendment note: This guide does not certify a newly enacted 2026 change, fee schedule or blanket LDA-consent requirement. Check the current Revenue Code, rules, amendments and official orders through the UP Board of Revenue, India Code and the competent authority. Planning requirements must be checked with the authority having jurisdiction over the parcel.

The practical benefit of the guide is a clear division of work: establish title and tenure, identify the revenue declaration route, examine planning controls and connect every approval to the exact land offered.

Revenue conversion and development permission are different

Permissions and records needed for a non-agricultural land project
Record or approvalMain purposeWhat it does not automatically provide
Section 80 declarationAddresses the statutory revenue treatment of the qualifying land and use described in the order.It does not independently sanction every subdivision, building, road or commercial activity proposed on the parcel.
Planning land-use positionEstablishes the land's treatment under the applicable master plan, zonal plan or other planning framework.It does not prove the applicant owns the land or that a private layout has been sanctioned.
Layout approvalAddresses the proposed subdivision and development under the competent authority's process and conditions.It does not extend automatically to adjoining khasra numbers or later changes absent the necessary approval.
Building sanctionApproves the relevant building proposal subject to the applicable rules and conditions.It does not turn an unapproved colony into an approved layout or resolve a defective title.

Access and infrastructure permissionsAddress lawful approach, services and other requirements relevant to the development.A road drawn in a brochure does not itself establish a legal right of way or infrastructure approval.

First identify the holding and the legal applicant

Start with the revenue village, tehsil, gata or khasra numbers, area, tenure and current khatauni. Compare these with the acquisition deed and the physical site. A village map can help connect the parcel to neighbouring plots and access, but it must be read alongside the title and measurement material.

Identify the person legally entitled to seek the declaration and the interest held. Joint ownership, succession, a partial share or an unresolved partition can affect the application and development structure. An intermediary's agreement to purchase does not automatically make that intermediary the recorded tenure holder or authorise every application.

Examine restrictions associated with the tenure, land category and transfer history. Public land, protected categories, acquisition issues or a recorded dispute cannot be treated as ordinary private conversion cases merely because surrounding land has been plotted.

How the conversion process should be approached

Identify the competent revenue authority and the application process applicable to the parcel. Use the current official procedure, required form and supporting documents. Application screens, document lists and payment demands should be checked against the actual service rather than copied from a private consultant's old template.

A complete application identifies the applicant, land, area and proposed use clearly. Where a report, inspection, notice, hearing or other inquiry is required under the governing framework, respond with consistent documents and retain acknowledgements. An application number is evidence of filing, not a declaration that has already been granted.

When an order is issued, obtain the complete text and schedules. Read the covered khasra numbers, area, declared use, conditions and operative directions. Check for subsequent corrections, modifications or proceedings affecting the order. The existence of a one-page screenshot does not establish the scope of the entire decision.

LDA jurisdiction and land-use checks

The Lucknow Development Authority's planning role must be examined by reference to its actual jurisdiction and the applicable planning instruments. A property described as “Lucknow” in an advertisement may fall within a different local or development authority arrangement. Establish the responsible body from official records.

For land within LDA jurisdiction, compare the parcel with the applicable master plan or other relevant planning material. Then identify whether the proposed project needs layout sanction, land-use permission, building approval or additional permissions. Revenue treatment and planning compliance should be recorded in separate columns of the file.

Questions to put to the planning authority before developing land
QuestionDetailed information neededWhy a Section 80 order alone is insufficient
Is this parcel inside your jurisdiction?Village, khasra numbers, mapped boundary and the authority position applicable to the site.A revenue order does not identify every planning body or jurisdictional condition governing development.
What is the applicable land use?The relevant plan, land-use designation and any lawful change or restriction affecting the parcel.Non-agricultural revenue treatment does not automatically permit every residential, commercial or industrial project.
Is this layout sanctioned?Approval number, date, complete sanctioned drawing, land schedule and conditions.A conversion order for a large holding does not itself sanction the promoter's subdivision into saleable plots.
Is my plot within the sanctioned boundary?Plot number, dimensions, roads and a parcel-to-layout comparison.A genuine approval for neighbouring land cannot be used to validate an extra phase or extension.
What remains to be complied with?Conditions concerning development, services, open spaces, access and other relevant matters.An approval document can impose obligations whose fulfilment must be checked separately.
Is the intended building permissible?Building rules, sanction requirements and applicable use or design controls.The revenue declaration is not the building plan or permission to start construction.

Fees, charges and processing time

Do not use a universal conversion percentage or a guaranteed completion period without the effective rule and the facts of the application. The applicable charges, assessment base, exemptions and administrative steps require verification from the current official framework and the competent office.

Ask for a written breakdown separating government charges, any authority development or permission charges, survey expenses and professional fees. Each government payment should be tied to an official demand or service reference. A consultant's lump-sum quotation is not proof of the statutory fee.

Processing depends on the completeness of the file and the inquiry required. Plan the purchase agreement around identifiable milestones—complete application, required authority response, actual order and approval conditions—rather than a promise that conversion will be “done soon”.

Buying a plot advertised as Section 80 converted

Obtain the order before paying a substantial deposit. Compare its land schedule with the seller's acquisition deed, current revenue entry, layout and the plot offered. The seller should be able to show how the small plot derives from the land covered by the order.

An order covering only part of a holding should not be represented as covering every plot in a colony. Likewise, a declaration in one person's name does not by itself explain later transfers, shares or subdivision. Trace the sequence and require the missing connecting documents.

Common mismatches and how to resolve them

A discrepancy between the revenue area and the sale schedule needs explanation through the underlying documents and measurement. Do not solve it by changing a number in the draft without establishing which record is correct. If a correction or demarcation is needed, complete the proper process.

Where the intended use conflicts with the planning position, identify the lawful route before selling or buying the land as ready for that use. A buyer should not be left with an unconditional price obligation while the seller retains an undefined promise to secure permission later.

If the conversion order has conditions, list them individually and record their status. “Order available” is an inadequate conclusion when important conditions remain unfulfilled. The agreement should allocate responsibility for obtaining documents and satisfying the identified requirements.

An example of a complete development file

Consider a private promoter offering residential plots carved out of a larger agricultural holding near Lucknow. A complete review connects the promoter's title, the exact converted area, the planning jurisdiction, sanctioned layout, individual plot and lawful approach road. No single document answers all six questions.

If the Section 80 order covers one parcel but the advertised plot lies partly on another, the title and approval file is incomplete. If the entire land is covered but the layout is not sanctioned where required, the revenue declaration still does not complete the planning analysis.

The useful output is a parcel-specific checklist showing what is established, which conditions remain and what must occur before payment or possession. Read the LDA approved plots guide for the next stage of the inquiry.

Frame the purchase agreement around the actual approval position

A purchaser can make a more informed decision when the agreement describes the present position accurately. If an application is pending, say so. If the order covers only part of the land, identify the covered area. If planning permission is still required, list it separately instead of describing the property as ready for unrestricted construction.

The agreement should connect each outstanding condition with a responsible person and an agreed milestone. That is particularly useful where the seller controls the application but the buyer would otherwise carry the price and development risk.

IssueSpecific condition to recordEvidence expected

Avoid a clause that merely says the seller will obtain “all approvals” without identifying what those are. The phrase may conceal different revenue, planning, building and infrastructure requirements. A document-based list lets the parties test completion objectively.

After an order is obtained, review the final deed and possession terms against it. The property description should remain consistent, and the buyer should receive the complete approval file rather than only a statement that the application has been disposed of. This creates a workable record for later financing, development and resale.

Frequently asked questions

Is Section 80 approval the same as a sanctioned layout?

No. They address different legal functions. Read the revenue declaration for its land and use, then examine the competent planning authority's requirements for the actual subdivision and development.

Can a Section 80 order cure a defective title?

It is not a substitute for ownership examination. The title chain, tenure restrictions, shares, litigation and authority to transfer still need to be established independently.

Can I rely on an online revenue entry mentioning conversion?

Use it as a lead to the underlying order. Obtain and read the complete order and land schedule, confirm its present status and compare it with the property being purchased.

Does every conversion application need the same LDA document?

Do not assume a universal answer. Establish the parcel's jurisdiction, current statutory and planning requirements and the actual proposed use. A claimed new requirement should be supported by its operative official text.

Official sources and related guides

Use the UP Board of Revenue and India Code to locate the governing revenue framework, UP Bhulekh for relevant records and the LDA website for authority information and planning services. Read our UP Bhulekh guide to assemble the parcel record.

A sound conversion decision keeps the revenue order, planning position, development approval, title and physical land identity connected. That approach explains exactly what the land can lawfully support and gives a purchaser meaningful completion conditions instead of a broad promise that “conversion is done”.
