Privacy policy

How information submitted through this website is handled.

This policy explains what information is collected through lexlawandtax.com and how it is used.

Information collected

The website collects only the information a visitor chooses to submit through the enquiry form, such as name, telephone number, email address and a description of the matter.

Use of information

Information submitted is used only to respond to the enquiry. It is not sold, rented or shared for marketing purposes.

Confidentiality

Enquiries are treated in confidence. Sending an enquiry does not by itself create an advocate–client relationship, and visitors are asked not to send confidential documents through this website.

Cookies and storage

This website does not set cookies for tracking or advertising. A single entry is kept in the browser’s local storage to record that the disclaimer acknowledgement has been shown, so that it is not repeated on every visit.

Fonts and images

Typefaces are served by Google Fonts and some photographs are served by Pexels. Requests to those services carry the visitor’s IP address, as any request to a third-party host does.

Retention

Enquiry records are retained only for as long as is necessary for the purpose for which they were submitted, or as required by law.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to the email address to be published.
